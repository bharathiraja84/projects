Review date: **2026-11-10**. This is a working compliance checklist, not legal advice or permission from World Headquarters. Club leadership must validate current policies and request clarification for any non-standard use.

## Findings and required implementation gates
- **Club, not an individual officer:** TMI Protocol 4.0 bars separate officer-related sites outside the club/Area/Division/District site. Club President/ExCom must own and approve the portal; technically this is the club site/member section, not a parallel VPE website. Confirm with Brand Team if operating alongside an existing public club site.
- **Website content:** official guidelines call for club name, current meeting time/location, contact, mission, link to District and toastmasters.org; publish accurate freshness dates. Include the mandatory disclaimer and TMI trademark ownership notice in site footer. Current exact wording must be reviewed before launch.
- **Brand system:** current brand manual (2025/26): Loyal Blue #004165; True Maroon #772432; Cool Gray #A9B2B1; Happy Yellow #F2DF74 as accent. Official typography, photography, clear space, allowed logo treatment and voice apply. Use official Brand Portal assets *only*; do not recreate, recolor or invent club logo or competing tagline. Do not commit official asset files here.
- **AI branding:** TMI logo must NOT be generated, recreated, modified by AI or uploaded into AI tools. An authorized non-AI design step applies actual logo to approved assets. Review any AI-generated published content for rights and required disclosure.
- **Copyright:** Do not rehost public TMI pages or republish educational PDFs. Do not copy membership-only, paid or Base Camp materials into RAG/vector stores. Link to official public resources, and use club-authored approved summaries instead.
- **AI and confidential information (additional hard restriction):** Current TMI governing AI guidance says not to input confidential Toastmasters-related information into AI tools, including nonpublic educational resources, financial information, internal documents or communications, or member/prospective-member information. Therefore NO Secretary weekly reports, ExCom minutes, club group histories, membership lists, or identifiable progress records may be sent to external AI APIs. The chatbot must start from nonconfidential, club-authored FAQ statements approved for AI processing and links to public TMI resources, without forwarding copyrighted page content. Obtain explicit clarification before any broader ingestion. Human-review all AI outputs before publishing official club communications.
- **Member information:** TMI governing guidance requires protection of member/prospective-member personal data. Apply access controls, purpose limitation, consent and applicable Indian privacy laws; prohibit exposing confidential Club Central details. Approval from an ExCom officer is NOT permission to redistribute proprietary TMI content.
- **Membership/education truth:** The local dashboard is club-maintained, not authoritative Club Central or Base Camp. No assumption of TMI-supported API access. Manual authorized data updates must be sourced, dated and audited.
- **Non-commercial club purpose:** Avoid promotions, solicitation, advertisements, unapproved partner endorsements or unrelated content. TMI website guidelines say individual members may not receive compensation to create/maintain/host a Toastmasters website, while reasonable site costs may be reimbursed; confirm applicability before any payment/consulting arrangement.
- **Guest versus member content:** public visitors should see approved club info only; private officer/participant data requires verified role-based access.
- **WhatsApp:** separate Meta approval/consent, no uncontrolled group-chat scraping; official member contact data cannot be used for unsolicited marketing.
